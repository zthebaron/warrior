# Chapter 3 — The Drifting Stones (`levels/03-drifting-stones.json`), measured

Chapter 3 adds **platform** and **dash** (Q5, `CHAPTER_MOVES_ADDED[3]`). It is greybox, difficulty 3, and has
5 segments with 4 torii. Built in parallel with chapter 2 (PR #12), against main's linter plus B-19's FACE_RISE
(PR #13). The level is generated by `tools/levelgen/03.mjs`, so the numbers stay in one place.

within 12 s, waiting included. HAZARD_INERT passes on segment 3: departing blind fails 4 of 8 arrival phases. The
linter passes with 0 errors and 0 warnings on main's rules and on B-19's FACE_RISE. There are no ASSIST_CASE
waypoints: no face is taller than +1.5.

## Design decisions the linter forced or allowed

- **Platform and dash are both taught from a torii.** CHAPTER_TEACH (D-015c) credits a teaching instance only if
  missing it cannot cost the segment, and a ride or a dash over a void can always fall. So T1 stands 1.8 u from
  stone_a's boarding point and T2 is the dash approach. A miss respawns you where you started.
- **The dash gaps sit under a lintel.** The first lintel ran 0.3 u past the landing lip, and the bot dashed in
  short and was pinned under it, so both lintels now stop 0.2 short of the lip. The bottom is 2.0 above the floor:
  a 1.9 capsule fits, and any jump hits it, even a tap (+0.995). That makes the dash necessary. A plain 1.5 u gap
  would be jumped, and the segment would teach nothing.
- **The gaps are 1.5 u, not 2.5.** The bot fires its dash about 1 u early (ground reach), and at 2.0 it caught the
  far lip and lost about 7 s. 1.5 leaves the 3.2 u dash about 0.7 u of margin.
- **The rides are 10 u over an 11.5 u void.** At 8 u the void was 9.5, exactly a double jump's reach (MOVE
  `double` maxH 9.5), so the platform could have been bypassed.
- **Each far ledge reaches 2 u under its slab's docked position.** With a 0.5 u overlap, a phase-sweep run
  stepped off before the slab docked and fell into the 1.5 u gap. PHASE_SWEEP caught it; one nav run had not.
- **The rock masses are at z −8, spaced 7 u** (chapters 1 and 2 use z −5.7, spaced 8.5). See OQ-060: in a chapter
  with climb and wall, the chaos stage climbed onto the old layout and wedged between two rocks.
